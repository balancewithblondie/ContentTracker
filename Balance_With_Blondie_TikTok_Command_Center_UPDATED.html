Pinterest board decoded

Colors

Deep forest green first; moss/sage second; cream, oatmeal, sand and warm taupe as breathing room; walnut/chocolate/rust for warmth; yellow gold as the only metallic accent.

Light + texture

Warm directional light, candle glow, dappled sunlight, wood, linen, woven baskets, plants, matte ceramics and natural textures. Avoid cool blue-white lighting and shiny plastic clutter.

Movement mood

Slow, intimate, grounded and feminine. More held poses, floor-level angles, hands/feet, breath, props and quiet transitions; less generic fitness-demo energy.

Visual rule: Aim for roughly 70% warm neutral/earth texture, 20% forest or moss green, and 10% gold/amber accent in the frame. Your green on-screen text boxes are already moving in the right direction—standardize them.
